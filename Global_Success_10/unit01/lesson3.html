Task 3 · Answer the questions 13 min

Scan the text · page 12

1. What do many people wrongly believe about housework? That housework is boring, and that it is only the duty of wives and mothers.

2. Why do some parents not ask their children to help? To give them more time to play or study.

3. What kinds of chores can teach life skills? Doing the laundry, cleaning the house, and taking care of other family members.

Task 3 · Answer the questions (2)

The two hardest questions

4. How does housework build character? Children learn to finish a task even when they do not enjoy doing it.

5. Why does sharing housework bring a family closer? Because children learn to appreciate how hard their parents work, and they share special moments together.

Notice

Questions 4 and 5 ask why, not what — your answer needs because or so that. Do not copy one line straight from the text.
