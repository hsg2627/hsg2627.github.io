2

Should · Shouldn’t

Sort the reasons, then complete the dialogue

25 minutes
Task 1 · Sort the reasons 10 min

Which column does each reason belong in?

  1. It develops life skills.
  2. It teaches responsibility.
  3. Children need time to play.
  4. Children may break things.
  5. It strengthens family bonds.
  6. Children need time to study.

Sort them: SHOULD or SHOULDN’T? SHOULD — 1, 2, 5  ·  SHOULDN’T — 3, 4, 6

Useful language

How to take a side politely

Giving an opinion

I strongly believe (that)…
In my opinion,…
It seems to me that…

Agreeing · disagreeing

I agree, but…
That’s a good point, however…
Exactly! · I’m afraid I don’t think so.

Notice

I agree, but… là cách bất đồng lịch sự nhất — công nhận trước, phản bác sau. Dùng nó nhiều hơn No, you’re wrong.

Task 2 · Listen and complete 15 min 🎧 Track 05 · 1:24

Complete the model conversation · page 12

1. Why does Anna think children should do housework? Doing housework helps them develop life skills.

2. What does Nam say children also need? They should be given plenty of playtime.
