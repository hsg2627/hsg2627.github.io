Grammar spotlight

Look at what you just matched

Compound sentence

Two clauses joined by and · but · or · so.

He is a talented artist, and he has received several awards.

To-infinitive

After verbs like start, decide, plan, want.

His mother started to upload his videos.

Three things, in this order

  • 👤

    Who

    Singer or band · what kind of music

    Writing? Singing? Which instruments?

  • ⭐

    Famous for

    Which song, show or award made them known

Digital & AI

Where did your facts come from?

10.C2.3 You may look your idol up with AI or a search engine before you talk. Say where the fact came from. “I read on their official page that…” is part of the answer, not an extra.

10.A3.1 Where can the class listen to them — legally? Spotify · Apple Music · YouTube Music · the artist’s own channel. Not a ripped file passed round the class.
