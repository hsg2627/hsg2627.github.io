Vocabulary

Five words for today

  • adopt (v) /əˈdɒpt/ áp dụng, bắt đầu thực hiện (lối sống, phương pháp)
  • awareness (of) (n) /əˈweənəs/ sự nhận thức, hiểu biết
  • carbon footprint (np) /ˌkɑːbən ˈfʊtprɪnt/ dấu chân các-bon — lượng khí thải mình gây ra
  • raise awareness (vp) /reɪz əˈweənəs/ nâng cao nhận thức
  • clean up (phr.v) /kliːn ʌp/ dọn dẹp, làm vệ sinh

Task 1 · Listen and read 6 min

Nam joins the Go Green Club · page 18

📖 Sách trang 18

Mở sách, nghe hai lượt rồi đọc lại theo cặp. Lượt đầu chỉ nghe, đừng nhìn sách.

Ba từ khoá cần bắt được khi nghe: set up (lập ra) · raise awareness (nâng cao nhận thức) · carbon footprint (dấu chân các-bon)

Task 2 · Lucky Number 10 min

Answer the questions · page 19

1. Who set up the Go Green Club? The Youth Union in Nam’s school set it up.

2. Why was it set up? To improve the environment and encourage a greener lifestyle.

3. What does the club plan to do? Organise more activities to raise awareness.

Task 2 · Lucky Number (2)

Three more questions

4. What is the first activity? Cleaning up the school right after the ceremony.

5. Why does Nam want to join? He is keen to reduce his carbon footprint.

6. What does Mai tell Nam at the end? The time and place of the club meeting.

Task 3 · Collocations 4 min

Match A with B · page 19

  • 1. raise c — awareness
  • 2. reduce d — your carbon footprint
  • 3. clean up e — the school
  • 4. adopt b — a greener lifestyle
  • 5. set up a — a club
Task 4 · Complete the sentences 4 min

Look back at the conversation

1. The club ______ (set up) by the Youth Union last year. was set up

2. We ______ (clean) the school right after the ceremony — it’s all planned. are / ’re going to clean

3. I think I ______ (join) the club. will / ’ll join

Grammar spotlight

Two futures, one passive

be going to

Kế hoạch đã định trước.

We are going to clean up the school.

will

Quyết định ngay lúc nói.

I think I ’ll join the club.

Passive

Khi ai làm không quan trọng bằng việc gì được làm: The club was set up by the Youth Union.
